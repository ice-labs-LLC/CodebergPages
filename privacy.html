Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 18, 2025

This Privacy Policy explains how Ice Labs ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information from and about you when you use our virtual reality games ("the Games"), available on the Meta Horizon Store and related Meta Platforms Technologies. This policy applies to all games published by Ice Labs.

Your use of our Games is also subject to the Meta Supplemental Terms of Service and Supplemental Meta Platforms Technologies Privacy Policy, which you should review.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide and improve our Games' functionality, to personalize your experience, and for other purposes described below.

A. Information Provided by You

  • Meta Horizon Profile Information: We access and collect your Meta Horizon Profile information, which may include your profile name, username, profile picture, and avatar.
  • In-Game Profile and Activity Data: When you play our Games, we collect information you provide directly, such as your in-game profile name, achievements, high scores, and interactions with other users.
  • Communications: If you communicate with us, we will collect the content of your communications and any contact information you provide.

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Device and Usage Data: We collect information about how you access and use our Games, including your in-game activity, the features you use, and the duration and frequency of your sessions.
  • Movement Data: Data related to the position and orientation of your headset and controllers.
  • Eye-Tracking Data: If you use a Meta Quest Pro and enable eye-tracking, some of our games may access abstracted gaze data. Raw image data is not collected or shared.
  • Natural Facial Expressions: If you use a Meta Quest Pro and enable Natural Facial Expressions, some of our games may access data about your facial expressions to animate your avatar.
  • Spatial Data: If our games use mixed reality features, we may, with your permission, collect data about the dimensions of your physical space.
  • Audio Data: If a game includes voice chat or microphone functionality, we may process audio data for communication purposes.

2. How We Use the Information We Collect

  • To provide and operate the Games and their features.
  • To personalize your experience.
  • To enable multiplayer functionality and social interactions.
  • To analyze and understand how the Games are used.
  • To communicate with you and provide customer support.
  • To ensure the security and integrity of the Games.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal data. We may share the information we collect with:

  • Other Users: In multiplayer or social settings.
  • Service Providers: Third parties who assist us in operating our Games.
  • Third-Party Apps: If you link your game account with a third-party service.
  • Legal Reasons: To comply with legal process or protect our rights.

4. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as necessary to provide the Games and fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

5. Your Rights and Choices

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of your personal data.
  • Right to Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data.
  • Right to Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Right to Object/Opt-out: Object to certain data processing.

To request data deletion, please send us a request that includes your username or account identifier.

6. Children's Privacy

Our Games are designed for users 10 and older. Under 13 requires a parent-managed Meta account. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 10.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the new policy on this page.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please refer to the contact information provided in the documentation for our games.
